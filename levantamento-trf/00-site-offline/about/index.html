Who is TRF Industrial Automation?

TRF Industrial Automation is an end-of-line automation partner based in Lenexa, Kansas, serving manufacturers in the United States. One team reviews the application, engineers and builds the system, installs it and supports it after startup, so one partner stands behind the scope TRF delivers.

TRF works one application at a time: a product, a rate, a pallet pattern and a plant floor, turned into a line that runs. The goal is a line that runs shift after shift.

  • Robotic palletizing: TRFCobot, TRFCompact, TRFSpeed and TRFSubzero, plus custom palletizers. See robotic palletizing.
  • Case packing: TRFRapidpack, TRFOnepack and TRFDroppack. See case packing.
  • Conveyor systems: TRFIntraflow and TRFCleanflow, plus end-of-line integration. See conveyor systems.

Who makes the TRF platforms?

The Torfresma group manufactures the TRF platforms. TRF Industrial Automation is Torfresma in the United States, responsible for each project in the United States from the first conversation to support after handover.

As part of the Torfresma group, TRF draws on the group's 30+ years of engineering experience, 350+ companies served and 2,000+ industrial lines designed and installed. TRF applies that experience to plants in the United States.

What problems does end-of-line automation solve?

End-of-line automation takes the most repetitive and hardest-to-staff work off the end of the line: manual palletizing, manual case packing and cases moved by hand between machines. The goal is a steady rate on every shift, with less strain on your people.

  • Hard-to-fill positions. Robotic palletizing and case packing take repetitive lifting off the schedule, so the line depends less on staffing the toughest jobs on the floor.
  • Ergonomics. Removing repeated lifting, bending and twisting at the end of the line takes that motion off your operators.
  • Line stops and backups. Conveying, accumulation and routing keep cases moving between machines.
  • Throughput. Each platform is matched to the rate, payload and pallet pattern of your line.
  • Floor space. Compact cells and engineered layouts make the most of the area you already have.
  • Changeovers and product mix. The real SKU mix is part of the application review, so the system fits the plant you run.

Can One Automation Partner Supply Palletizing, Case Packing and Conveyors?

Yes. TRF supplies case packing, conveying and robotic palletizing as one engineered end-of-line system: one project, one engineering team and one partner for the scope TRF delivers. A plant can also start with a single platform, such as a TRFCobot on one line, and add a case packer and conveyors later with the same partner.
