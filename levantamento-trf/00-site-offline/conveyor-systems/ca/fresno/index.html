What is the difference between a sanitary conveyor and a standard industrial conveyor?

A sanitary conveyor is designed around cleaning and food contact, not only transport. It carries exposed food between processing, inspection and packaging, with open construction that is easy to sanitize. A standard industrial conveyor moves packaged goods, such as sealed cases, cartons, trays and totes, where flow, routing and accumulation matter more than washdown.

TRF covers both with two platforms. TRFCleanflow is the sanitary platform for exposed food, and TRFIntraflow is the modular platform for packaged goods moving between packaging, case packing, palletizing and the warehouse.

Automation for California food and consumer goods plants

  • Dairy and cheese: exposed product between processing and packaging on TRFCleanflow, and packaged products toward case packing and palletizing on TRFIntraflow.

  • Frozen food: conveying in cold production areas, with TRFSubzero for palletizing below 32°F.
  • Protein processing: beef, pork, poultry and seafood, with the TRFCleanflow Washdown line where washdown is frequent.

How do you choose between TRFIntraflow and TRFCleanflow?

Start from what is on the conveyor and how the area is cleaned. Use TRFCleanflow when the conveyed item is exposed food or the conveyor sits in a hygienic or washdown zone. Use TRFIntraflow when the item is a case, carton, tray, tote or other packaged unit moving between automation processes.

Platform Product and zone Typical functions Best fit
TRFIntraflow Packaged goods: cases, cartons, trays, totes Transport, accumulate, merge, divert, sort, elevate Secondary packaging to palletizing and warehouse interfaces
TRFCleanflow Dry Exposed food in dry, low-moisture areas Convey, incline, transfer, distribute, merge Processed foods cleaned by vacuum, brushing and wipe-down
TRFCleanflow Washdown Exposed food in washdown areas Convey, incline, transfer, distribute, merge Beef, pork, poultry and seafood lines with frequent washdown

TRFCleanflow construction follows design targets for each project: 304 stainless steel (316 optional on Washdown for aggressive environments), IP65 electrical components as a baseline on Dry, and IP66/IP69K components in direct washdown zones with NEMA 4X control enclosures on Washdown. When a project crosses both environments, the two platforms are engineered as one coordinated material-flow system.

Can a conveyor system integrate with your palletizer and case packer?

Yes. TRFIntraflow includes system integration modules: sensors, PLC and HMI, barcode and vision interfaces, machine communication, palletizer interfaces and warehouse interfaces where applicable. That is how conveying connects the case packer upstream and the palletizer downstream into one flow.

TRF also supplies the machines at both ends: case packing platforms that deliver the sealed case and robotic palletizing platforms that build the pallet. When a California project buys all three as one scope, end-of-line integration puts it under one engineering plan and one responsible partner.

What to prepare before requesting a technical consultation in Fresno

An application review moves faster when a few details are ready in advance:

  • Products conveyed: cases, cartons, trays, totes or exposed food, with dimensions and typical weight.
  • Flow map: where product enters and exits the system, and which machines sit at each end.
  • Rates: average and peak volume per line.
  • Environment and sanitation: whether the area is dry, low-moisture or washed down, and the cleaning routine used today.
  • Layout: available floor space, level changes and access for maintenance and sanitation.
  • Existing equipment: any case packer, palletizer or warehouse system the conveyor interfaces with.

How Does Zero-Pressure Accumulation Work on a Case Conveyor?

Zero-pressure accumulation divides the conveyor into zones that start and stop individually, so units accumulate without pressing against each other. When the downstream machine is ready, zones release in sequence. It protects products and cases, helps prevent jams at transfers and gives a palletizer a predictable infeed. TRFIntraflow includes zero-pressure accumulation, metering and gapping modules.
