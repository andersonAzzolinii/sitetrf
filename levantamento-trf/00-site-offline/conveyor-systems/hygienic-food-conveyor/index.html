Conveyor Systems / TRFCleanflow

TRFCleanflow sanitary conveyor systems are clean by design and robust by nature.

Sanitary conveying designed for food-contact zones and the cleaning routines that come with them.

TRFCleanflow is the sanitary conveyor systems platform of TRF Industrial Automation, built to move exposed food between processing, inspection and packaging, in a Dry line for low-moisture areas and a Washdown line for high-pressure, high-temperature cleaning.

What is a sanitary conveyor, and how is it different from a standard industrial conveyor?

A sanitary conveyor, also called a hygienic conveyor, is designed so that every surface the product touches, and every surface that can collect soil, can be cleaned and inspected as part of the plant's sanitation routine. In a food plant the conveyor sits inside the hygienic zone, so it is judged by how fast and how completely it can be cleaned, not only by how much product it moves.

The difference from a standard industrial conveyor is the whole architecture, not the motor or the belt alone: open frames instead of closed boxes, sloped and self-draining surfaces instead of flat ledges, continuous welds instead of crevices, and belts and guides that come off for cleaning. A food grade conveyor system adds food-grade belting: on the Washdown line, the hygienic conveyor belts are modular plastic or monolithic, selected for the product.

TRFCleanflow extends TRF automation upstream into food processing. It is designed around sanitation, accessibility, product integrity and uptime rather than transportation alone, and each stainless steel conveyor path is engineered as one hygienic system that conveys, inclines, transfers, distributes and merges product.

Which applications fit sanitary conveyor systems?

TRFCleanflow fits applications where sanitation or product handling limits production. Typical applications:

  • Processing to packaging transfer of exposed cuts, portions or prepared foods.
  • Inspection feeds, carrying product from processing to inspection before packaging.
  • Distribution of one product stream to several packing stations.
  • Merging product from several processing points into one packaging line.
  • Inclines and declines between processing and packaging equipment.
  • Wet and cold production areas, where stainless construction has to withstand repeated washdown cycles.

The platform is built for mid-sized and large food processors in protein processing (beef, pork, poultry and seafood), frozen food, dairy and cheese and food processing, including ready-to-eat products, wherever sanitation, washdown, product integrity and uptime are critical.

Is your line ready for a sanitary conveyor system?

Your line is a strong candidate for TRFCleanflow when most of these statements are true:

  • Exposed food moves between processing, inspection and packaging.
  • Sanitation time limits how soon the line returns to production.
  • Product tips, sticks or breaks at transfer points.
  • Some areas are cleaned daily with water and chemicals, while others are cleaned dry.
  • Existing conveyors trap water or product, or are hard to reach for cleaning.
  • You want one partner from engineering to commissioning.

Before quoting, TRF reviews five inputs with your team: the product (dimensions, weight, temperature and condition), the process rate, layout drawings with ceiling heights and drain positions, your sanitation program and chemicals, and the equipment the conveyor feeds or receives from.

How do you choose between TRFIntraflow, TRFCleanflow dry and TRFCleanflow washdown?

Choose by what is conveyed and how each zone is cleaned. TRFCleanflow Dry fits industrialized foods with moderate, infrequent cleaning by vacuum, brushing and wipe-down. TRFCleanflow Washdown fits animal protein and similar products with daily sanitation using chemicals, high pressure and high temperature. TRFIntraflow takes over once the product is packed.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can a Conveyor System Be Designed for Both Dry and Washdown Zones in the Same Plant?

Yes. A plant can have a wet processing area washed every day and a dry packaging area cleaned without water. TRF engineers TRFCleanflow Dry for low-moisture areas and TRFCleanflow Washdown for frequent, high-pressure cleaning within one project, and defines during engineering where construction changes, how the boundary is cleaned and how the flow continues into packaging and case handling.

How Much Does a Stainless Steel Conveyor Cost?

The cost depends on the product and its condition, the line selected for each zone, conveyor length, inclines and transfers, belt type, stainless grade, drives and controls, and the sanitation routine the line must support. TRFCleanflow is always quoted as a complete engineered system, so a TRF Sales Engineer defines the scope from your product, layout and cleaning program.

Can a Single Supplier Engineer, Build and Install My Entire Conveyor System?

Yes. TRFCleanflow projects are delivered turnkey: one partner is responsible for engineering, manufacturing, controls, installation and commissioning. TRF also supplies TRFIntraflow for packaged goods, plus case packing and palletizing, so a plant can have one design and one partner from the processing room to the finished pallet. Each project is scoped from your product, layout and sanitation program.
