One partner for the whole end of line, from product to pallet

The end of the line starts at the product. The product goes into the case, and the case goes onto the pallet, so TRF plans the whole journey as one system.

End-of-line problems often come from the gaps between machines, not from a single machine: a case packer that outruns the conveyor, an accumulation zone that cannot absorb a pallet change, or three suppliers who each point to another when the line stops. TRF addresses those gaps by delivering case packing, conveying and robotic palletizing as one engineered system: one project, one engineering team and one partner for the scope TRF delivers.

TRF manages engineering, manufacturing, controls, assembly, integration and commissioning within one coordinated delivery model. The plant can also start with a single platform, such as a TRFCobot replacing manual palletizing on one line, and add a case packer and conveyors later without starting over with a new supplier or a new controls philosophy.
