## How an End-of-Line Automation Project Runs With TRF

One TRF team looks after every phase, from the first application review to the day the line runs: project management, engineering and build, testing at the TRF facility, installation, training and support.

1. **Application review and validation**
    - Sales engineering reviews your application
    - Products and platforms matched to the job
    - Rates confirmed against your line
    - Layout validated for your floor space
2. **Quote and project kickoff**
    - Quote built on the validated application
    - Kickoff and project meetings with your team
    - Approvals before the build starts
3. **Project management, engineering and build**
    - A project manager coordinating the schedule
    - Engineering and design of the system
    - Manufacturing and assembly
4. **FAT, installation, startup and SAT**
    - FAT (Factory Acceptance Test): your equipment is tested at the TRF facility
    - Installation and startup by TRF
    - SAT (Site Acceptance Test) at your plant
5. **Training, handover and ongoing support**
    - Operator and maintenance training
    - Handover to your team
    - Technical support after startup
    - Preventive maintenance (PM) program with TRFCare

## One Partner for the Whole End of Line, From Product to Pallet

The end of the line starts at the product. The product goes into the case, and the case goes onto the pallet, so TRF plans the whole journey as one system.

End-of-line problems often come from the gaps between machines, not from a single machine: a case packer that outruns the conveyor, an accumulation zone that cannot absorb a pallet change, or three suppliers who each point to another when the line stops. TRF addresses those gaps by delivering [case packing](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/), [conveying](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/) and [robotic palletizing](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/) as one engineered system: one project, one engineering team and one partner for the scope TRF delivers.

TRF manages engineering, manufacturing, controls, assembly, integration and commissioning within one coordinated delivery model. The plant can also start with a single platform, such as a TRFCobot replacing manual palletizing on one line, and add a case packer and conveyors later without starting over with a new supplier or a new controls philosophy.
