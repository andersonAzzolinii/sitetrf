## The Nine TRF Platforms Available to Every Industry

Every industry page presents all nine platforms, because the platform is chosen by line setup and speed rather than by sector. They are organized in three product groups: [robotic palletizing](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/), [case packing](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/) and [conveyor systems](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/).

### Palletizing Platforms

- **[TRFCobot](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/cobot-palletizer/)**: collaborative palletizing for lower-speed, high-mix lines with limited floor space.
- **[TRFCompact](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/compact-palletizer/)**: an industrial robot in a compact, standardized cell, for more speed and duty cycle without a large footprint.
- **[TRFSpeed](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/high-speed-palletizer/)**: high-performance palletizing for high-volume, continuous and multi-line production.
- **[TRFSubzero](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/subzero-palletizer/)**: palletizing for areas below 32°F, in frozen food and ice cream.

### Case Packing Platforms

- **[TRFRapidpack](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/robotic-case-packer/)**: flexible robotic top-load packing for high-mix lines and delicate or irregular products.
- **[TRFOnepack](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/integrated-case-packer/)**: case erecting, robotic packing and sealing in one machine.
- **[TRFDroppack](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/drop-packer/)**: gravity case packing for repetitive flexible packages, such as VFFS, IQF and frozen food bags.

### Conveying Platforms

- **[TRFIntraflow](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/case-conveyor-intralogistics/)**: case intralogistics for packaged goods between packaging, case packing and palletizing.
- **[TRFCleanflow](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/hygienic-food-conveyor/)**: hygienic conveying of exposed food, with a [Washdown line](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/hygienic-food-conveyor/washdown/) and a [Dry line](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/hygienic-food-conveyor/dry/).

Beyond the platforms, a [custom palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/custom-palletizer/) covers products, loads or layouts outside the standard ranges, and [end-of-line integration](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/end-of-line-integration/) connects case packing, conveying and palletizing as one system in any industry.

## Which Platform Fits Each Step of the End of Line?

Each step from exposed product to finished pallet has its own TRF option, and a plant can automate one step or the whole sequence.

| Step | What happens | TRF options |
|---|---|---|
| Exposed product handling | Product moves between processing, inspection and primary packaging | TRFCleanflow Dry or Washdown |
| Case forming and sealing | Flat cases are erected and closed | Inside TRFOnepack or an end-of-line integration project |
| Case packing | Products are grouped and loaded into cases from the top | TRFRapidpack, TRFOnepack or TRFDroppack |
| Case conveying | Cases are accumulated, merged, diverted, elevated and routed | TRFIntraflow |
| Palletizing | Cases, trays or bundles are stacked on pallets | TRFCobot, TRFCompact, TRFSpeed, TRFSubzero or a custom palletizer |
| Wrapping, labeling and warehouse interfaces | Finished pallets leave the line | End-of-line integration |

## How Do You Choose Packaging Automation for Your Industry?

Start from your line, not the sector: the industry sets the conditions and the line sets the platform. Screen four variables before you shortlist equipment.

1. **Speed.** Cases per minute decides among palletizers, and products per minute among case packers, using the figures in the table above.
2. **Load and product behavior.** Case weight sets the palletizing platform. Repetitive flexible bags point to gravity packing, while delicate, variable or mixed products point to robotic packing.
3. **Space and layout.** Brownfield plants with tight packaging areas favor compact cells and machines that combine functions, such as TRFOnepack. Multi-line plants favor a centralized palletizing area fed by TRFIntraflow.
4. **Environment.** Exposed food and washdown zones point to TRFCleanflow, palletizing areas below 32°F to TRFSubzero, and packaged-goods zones to TRFIntraflow.

When more than one platform could work, choose the one that matches your throughput, production intensity and growth plans, not simply the lowest initial investment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Industries Need Sanitary Conveyors?

Industries that move exposed food or run conveyors in hygienic and washdown zones. TRFCleanflow lists beef, pork, poultry, seafood, frozen foods, ready-to-eat products, and dairy and cheese among its priority industries. Where the item conveyed is a case, carton, tray or tote, the platform is TRFIntraflow instead, and a project that crosses both zones combines the two platforms in one coordinated material-flow system.

### Which Industries Palletize Below Freezing?

Frozen food, ice cream, frozen prepared foods and the frozen areas of meat processing plants. For palletizing areas below 32°F, the platform is [TRFSubzero](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/subzero-palletizer/), the TRF palletizer built for those areas.

### Can One Automation Partner Supply Palletizing, Case Packing and Conveyors?

Yes. TRF supplies case packing, conveying and palletizing as one integrated end-of-line solution: TRFRapidpack, TRFOnepack or TRFDroppack pack the cases, TRFIntraflow moves and routes them, and TRFCobot, TRFCompact, TRFSpeed or TRFSubzero build the pallet. With [end-of-line integration](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/end-of-line-integration/), one partner is responsible for engineering and integration from layout to startup.

### How Does Automation Help With Labor Shortages in Food Plants?

Automation takes over the repetitive end-of-line tasks that are hardest to staff: grouping and loading products into cases, moving cases by hand and building pallets. Operators move to supervision, quality checks and changeovers, and the line no longer depends on how fast people can lift. Repetitive lifting, bending and twisting also leave the end of the line.

### How Do I Estimate the Payback of Automation in My Plant?

For palletizing, use the [palletizing ROI calculator](https://trfautomation.com/tools/palletizing-roi-calculator/). The estimate divides the system cost, including maintenance, by the annual net savings: the labor cost of manual palletizing minus the electricity the robot uses. The result is an initial planning estimate that a TRF Sales Engineer completes with the costs of your project.
