# Frozen Food Packaging Automation for IQF Bags, Frozen Meals and Ice Cream, From Case Packing to Pallet

Automate IQF bags, frozen meals and ice cream from case packing to the pallet, including below-freezing applications.

Source: https://trfautomation.com/industries/frozen-food-and-ice-cream/

Frozen food packaging automation from TRF Industrial Automation is the case packing, conveying and palletizing equipment that moves frozen products and ice cream from primary packaging to a finished pallet, including robotic palletizing in areas below 32°F with TRFSubzero.

What separates it from ambient packaging automation is temperature: part of the line, and sometimes the palletizing area itself, runs below freezing, which changes how equipment is selected and how operators work. TRF works after the freezer and after primary packaging; freezers, IQF tunnels and primary packaging machines stay with their suppliers. The pains are familiar: crews that are hard to hire and keep in a freezer, manual pallet building that strains people, and an end of line that cannot keep pace with freezing.

## Which Palletizer Fits an Ice Cream or Frozen Food Line?

The answer depends less on the product than on where the pallet is built. When the palletizing area itself sits below 32°F, the TRF line points to TRFSubzero. Its target applications include frozen food production lines, ice cream manufacturing and end-of-line palletizing, and frozen prepared foods. Outside the cold area, TRFCobot, TRFCompact or TRFSpeed are selected by cases per minute, case weight, floor space and SKU mix.

Cold room palletizing also takes people out of a demanding position on a frozen line: repetitive lifting, bending and twisting. TRF's published [Custom Foods case study](https://trfautomation.com/resources/case-studies/custom-foods/) documents robotic palletizing below freezing in De Soto, Kansas.

## Can a Case Packer Handle Frozen or IQF Products?

Yes. TRFDroppack packs IQF product bags and frozen food bags: it collates flexible packages and loads them into the case with a controlled drop, without robotic end-of-arm tooling. That makes it the first platform to evaluate for IQF bag case packing when the bag and the pack pattern are repetitive.

Frozen meals and ice cream bring cartons, trays, cups, tubs and multipacks, sometimes on the same line. TRFRapidpack covers that variety with recipe-based changeovers and controlled handling for delicate or irregular items, and frozen food is among its focus industries. Smaller frozen and ice cream lines that would otherwise buy an erector, a packer and a sealer can look at TRFOnepack. TRF confirms the platform with your own frozen packages before selection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a Robotic Palletizer Operate Below Freezing (32°F)?

Yes, with equipment built for it. TRFSubzero is the TRF robotic palletizer for areas below 32°F, positioned for frozen food production lines, ice cream manufacturing, frozen prepared foods and meat processing areas. TRF validates each application with your product, end-of-arm tool and pallet pattern.

### How Does Ice Cream Palletizing Differ From Other Frozen Palletizing?

The formats differ more than the principle. Ice cream arrives in cups, tubs, cartons and multipacks, which are packed top-load into cases by TRFRapidpack or TRFOnepack before palletizing. The palletizer then depends on the temperature of the area: TRFSubzero below 32°F, and TRFCobot, TRFCompact or TRFSpeed outside the cold area, chosen by cases per minute and case weight.

### What Conveyors Are Used in a Frozen Food Plant?

Two kinds. TRFCleanflow carries exposed product between processing, inspection and packaging, with a Washdown line for zones cleaned daily and a Dry line for low-moisture areas. TRFIntraflow carries cases, cartons, trays and totes between packing, palletizing and warehouse operations, with accumulation, merging, diverting, sorting and elevation modules. TRF engineers both as systems, with controls included.

### Can One Palletizer Serve Several Frozen Packaging Lines?

Yes, when the combined rate and the layout allow it. TRFSpeed scales from a dedicated cell to a centralized system that serves several production lines, and TRFIntraflow merges, routes and accumulates cases from each line so the palletizer receives an organized flow. In frozen plants, sharing one palletizing area also reduces how much equipment has to sit inside a cold area.

### Can One Automation Partner Supply Palletizing, Case Packing and Conveyors for a Frozen Food Plant?

Yes. TRF supplies case packing, conveying and palletizing as one integrated end-of-line solution: TRFDroppack, TRFRapidpack or TRFOnepack pack the cases, TRFIntraflow moves and routes them, and TRFCobot, TRFCompact, TRFSpeed or TRFSubzero build the pallet. One partner is responsible from layout to startup, and [Parts and Service](https://trfautomation.com/parts-and-service/) supports TRF-supplied equipment afterward.

### How Is Payback Estimated for Frozen Food Packaging Automation?

Payback depends on the labor hours the system replaces, shifts per day, local labor cost and the installed cost of the system. The estimate follows the method of the A3 (Association for Advancing Automation) robot ROI calculator: the system cost, including 20 years of maintenance at 5% per year, divided by the annual net savings, which is the labor cost of manual palletizing minus the electricity the robot uses. The [palletizing ROI calculator](https://trfautomation.com/tools/palletizing-roi-calculator/) applies this method to your location, your shifts and the people who palletize by hand today.
