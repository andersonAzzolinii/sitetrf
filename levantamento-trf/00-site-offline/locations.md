## Do You Need an Automation Supplier With an Office Near Your Plant?

Not necessarily. What matters is how support reaches your plant when the line needs it. TRF support starts remotely: a technician works the issue with your own team over a call or a remote connection, without waiting for a trip. When hands-on work is needed, a technician travels from Lenexa to the plant.

Customers who already run TRF equipment find the full support model, the spare parts process and the TRFCare preventive maintenance program on the [Parts and Service](https://trfautomation.com/parts-and-service/) page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who Provides Robotic Palletizing Near Me?

TRF Industrial Automation supplies robotic palletizing to plants across the United States from its base in Lenexa, Kansas, so you do not need to be near a local office. The platforms are TRFCobot, TRFCompact, TRFSpeed and TRFSubzero, plus custom palletizers, all compared on the [robotic palletizing page](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/).

### Who Supplies Case Packing Equipment for Food and CPG Manufacturers in the US?

TRF Industrial Automation supplies case packing equipment to food and CPG manufacturers in the United States: TRFRapidpack for flexible robotic packing, TRFOnepack to erect, pack and seal the case in one machine, and TRFDroppack for controlled gravity loading of flexible packages. See the [case packing page](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/).
