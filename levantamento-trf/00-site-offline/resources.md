## How Much Does a Robotic Palletizer Cost?

TRF publishes three reference list prices, all EX Works Lenexa, Kansas, installation not included: US$ 120,000 for the [TRFCobot](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/cobot-palletizer/) Dual, US$ 180,000 for the [TRFCompact](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/compact-palletizer/) Dual and US$ 350,000 for the [TRFSpeed](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/high-speed-palletizer/) 100. Installation, line integration and options are quoted with your application, because four things move the total: line speed, product and case weight, pallet pattern and the layout of your end-of-line area.

The article [How Much Does a Robotic Palletizer Cost?](https://trfautomation.com/resources/blog/how-much-does-a-robotic-palletizer-cost/) breaks the investment into the items every palletizing project contains, so your team can compare proposals on the same scope. The [robotic palletizer cost page](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/robotic-palletizer-cost/) explains the cost drivers next to the payback calculator.

## What Is the Typical Payback Period for a Robotic Palletizer?

Payback depends on the labor hours the palletizer replaces, shifts per day, local labor cost and the installed cost of the system. The [palletizing ROI calculator](https://trfautomation.com/tools/palletizing-roi-calculator/) applies that method to your platform, your state, the people per shift on palletizing, hours per shift, shifts per day, days per week and the investment you want to test.

The result is an initial estimate, not a quote: a TRF Sales Engineer completes it with your data.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do You Choose a Palletizing System?

Choose by line speed, case weight and floor space. TRFCobot covers about 1 to 10 cases per minute with cases up to about 22 lb, TRFCompact about 5 to 15 cases per minute up to about 44 lb, and TRFSpeed about 10 to 35 cases per minute up to about 66 lb. TRFSubzero covers palletizing areas below 32°F. TRF engineering confirms the final capacity for each application.

### Can a Palletizer Be Integrated With My Existing Conveyor and Wrapping Line?

Yes. TRF engineers palletizing, case packing and conveying as one end-of-line system, supported from Lenexa, Kansas. TRFSpeed is built to work with conveyors, accumulation, pallet handling, slip sheets, wrapping, labeling and warehouse interfaces, and [end-of-line integration](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/end-of-line-integration/) puts one partner in charge of the whole flow from case packer to pallet.

### Who Makes Robotic Case Palletizers With Local Support in the US?

TRF designs, builds, installs and supports robotic palletizers for manufacturers across the United States. The family covers TRFCobot, TRFCompact, TRFSpeed and TRFSubzero, plus a [custom palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/custom-palletizer/) for products or layouts outside the standard ranges. How TRF supports its equipment after startup, including spare parts, is described on the [Parts and Service](https://trfautomation.com/parts-and-service/) page.
