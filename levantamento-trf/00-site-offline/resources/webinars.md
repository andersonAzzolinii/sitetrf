## Which Questions Should You Bring to a Supplier Conversation?

Agree on them with your team first, so the conversation covers what each department needs:

- Which platform fits our product, speed and layout, and why not the next one up or down?
- What end-of-arm tool handles our heaviest and most difficult product?
- How are recipes and pallet patterns changed, and who on our team can do it?
- How does the system connect to our conveyors, labelers and wrappers?
- What does installation and startup involve, and what must our team prepare?
- How is the equipment supported after startup?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Does a Robotic Palletizer Handle SKU or Pallet Pattern Changeovers?

A changeover usually means selecting a stored recipe that holds the case dimensions, pick logic and pallet pattern for the new SKU. When the new case fits the same end-of-arm tool, the change happens mostly in software. [TRFCobot](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/cobot-palletizer/) supports multiple recipes and pallet patterns, which is why it fits high-mix lines with frequent changeovers.

### What Floor Space and Reach Do I Need to Validate Before Buying a Cobot Palletizer?

Validate the area for the robot, the infeed pick point, one or more pallet positions and operator access to remove full pallets. Then confirm reach: the robot must place the farthest case on the top layer of your tallest pallet. Pallet height and case size change the answer, so TRF validates reach, end-of-arm tool and pallet pattern for each application.
