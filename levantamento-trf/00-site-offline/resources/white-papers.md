## How Do You Choose Between TRFCobot, TRFCompact and TRFSpeed?

Choose by line speed, case weight and floor space. The three platforms form one family, and the right model is defined by your line setup and rate rather than by the size of the robot.

| Platform | Typical speed | Case weight | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [TRFCobot](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/cobot-palletizer/) | About 1 to 10 cases per minute | Up to about 22 lb | Lower-speed, high-mix lines with limited floor space |
| [TRFCompact](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/compact-palletizer/) | About 5 to 15 cases per minute | Up to about 44 lb | Industrial robot and higher duty cycle in a compact standardized cell |
| [TRFSpeed](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/high-speed-palletizer/) | About 10 to 35 cases per minute | Up to about 66 lb | High volume, continuous or multi-line palletizing |

*Typical ranges. TRF engineering confirms final capacity for each application (product, end-of-arm tool, pallet pattern, reach and process).*

The ranges overlap on purpose. When more than one platform could work, choose the one that matches the throughput, production intensity and growth your line needs, not only the lowest initial investment. Palletizing areas below 32°F point to TRFSubzero, and products or layouts outside the standard ranges point to a [custom palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/custom-palletizer/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is the Difference Between a Dedicated Palletizing Cell and a Centralized Multi-Line System?

A dedicated cell palletizes the output of one production line, so each line runs on its own. A centralized multi-line system brings several lines into one palletizing area, which changes the conveying, accumulation and pallet handling around it. TRFSpeed is configurable and modular, so it scales from a dedicated cell to a centralized system serving multiple lines.

### What Is Slip Sheet Palletizing and When Is It Needed?

Slip sheet palletizing places a thin sheet between layers or under the load. Plants use slip sheets when a pallet load needs more stability or protection, or when their own customers require them. Slip sheet handling is one of the TRFSpeed options, alongside multipick, automatic pallet handling and pallet discharge.
