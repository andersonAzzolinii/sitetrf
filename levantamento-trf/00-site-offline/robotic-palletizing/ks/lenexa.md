## What Is a Robotic Palletizer?

A robotic palletizer takes finished cases from a conveyor and stacks them on a pallet in a programmed pattern, layer by layer, until the load is complete. The robot, collaborative or industrial, carries an end-of-arm tool selected for the case type, size, weight and surface.

Each pallet pattern is stored as a recipe with the case dimensions, the pick logic and the layer arrangement. A plant running several SKUs switches recipes when the product changes instead of rebuilding the process, and operators move from lifting cases to supervising the line.

## What to Prepare Before Requesting a Technical Consultation in Lenexa

An application review moves faster when a few details are ready in advance:

- **Product and packaging:** case dimensions, weight in lb and how the case behaves when gripped.
- **Line rate:** cases per minute, and how many lines feed the palletizing area.
- **Pallet requirements:** pallet type and size, the pattern for each SKU and whether slip sheets are used.
- **Floor space:** the area available for the cell and access for forklifts or pallet trucks.
- **Operating environment:** shifts per day, changeover frequency and whether the area is below freezing.
- **Existing equipment:** any case packer, conveyor or stretch wrapper the palletizer connects to.

### What Is the Difference Between a Cobot Palletizer and a Traditional Robotic Palletizer?

A cobot palletizer uses a collaborative robot, designed for shared workspaces at moderate speeds and lighter payloads. A traditional robotic palletizer uses an industrial robot with higher speed, payload and duty cycle, usually as a dedicated cell. TRFCobot is the cobot option, while TRFCompact and TRFSpeed use industrial robots for higher rates.

### How Does a Robotic Palletizer Handle SKU or Pallet Pattern Changeovers?

Pallet patterns are stored as recipes, so changing SKU usually means selecting the stored recipe instead of making mechanical adjustments. The limit is usually the end-of-arm tool: one tool has to handle every case in the mix, or the cell needs a tool change. TRF reviews the full SKU and pattern list during the application review.
