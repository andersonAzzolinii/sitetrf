## What Is an Integrated Case Packing Machine, and How Is It Different From Separate Erecting, Packing and Sealing Machines?

An integrated case packing machine erects the case from a flat blank, loads the products into it and seals it inside one coordinated system. It covers the whole secondary packaging step, from flat case to sealed case, ready for conveying and palletizing. Form, pack, seal: three processes, one solution.

The alternative is a line of separate equipment: a case erector, then a case packer machine, then a case sealer, each with its own frame, controls and interfaces. That arrangement works, but it multiplies the interfaces to specify, install, connect and support, and it takes more floor space.

| What you compare | Three separate machines | TRFOnepack |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment interfaces | Erector to packer to sealer, each connection designed and commissioned | One machine, fewer interfaces and integration points |
| Controls | One control system per machine, plus line coordination | The three processes managed as one coordinated system |
| Floor space | Three frames plus the conveyors between them | Several packaging functions in one machine architecture |
| Operation | Operators follow three machines and their handoffs | Operators follow one machine |
| Responsibility | Several suppliers, schedules and acceptance steps | One capital project with one responsible partner |

Inside the machine, product loading is robotic, so integration does not lock the line to a single format. The trade-off is scope and speed: lines that need higher rates, vision-guided picking or a very wide range of formats belong with a dedicated robotic cell such as [TRFRapidpack](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/robotic-case-packer/).

## How Do You Choose Between TRFRapidpack, TRFOnepack and TRFDroppack?

Choose by rate, product behavior and how much of the case process belongs in one machine. The three TRF case packing platforms form one family, and line setup and speed define the model.

| Platform | Application range | Packing principle | Typical fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [TRFRapidpack](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/robotic-case-packer/) | About 100 to 200 products per minute | Delta robots, vision-guided or indexed picking | High-mix lines, multiple SKUs, variable product flow |
| TRFOnepack | About 20 to 50 products per minute | Case erecting, robotic packing and case sealing in one machine | Small and mid-sized plants consolidating secondary packaging |
| [TRFDroppack](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/drop-packer/) | About 20 to 500 products per minute | Controlled gravity loading | Repetitive flexible packages and standardized patterns |

Two lines at the same rate can land on different platforms. A plant with no case erector on the floor and a standard pattern is a strong TRFOnepack candidate, because the machine brings the missing processes with it. A plant that already erects and seals its cases, and changes formats often, may be better served by a dedicated robotic cell. The [case packing page](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/) and the [top-load case packer guide](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/top-load-case-packer/) compare the family in more detail.

Behind the machine, [TRFIntraflow](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/case-conveyor-intralogistics/) conveys the sealed cases and [TRFCobot](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/cobot-palletizer/), [TRFCompact](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/compact-palletizer/) or [TRFSpeed](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/high-speed-palletizer/) build the pallet, so the whole end of line comes from one partner.

### What Floor Space Does an Integrated Case Packing Machine Require?

The space depends on the case formats, the product infeed and how the machine sits in your line. ### How Much Does a Case Packing Machine Cost?

The cost depends on your products, case formats, rates and the integration scope around the machine. TRFOnepack combines erecting, packing and sealing in one capital project, so the scope is defined once instead of three times. Payback comes from the manual labor removed across those three steps, the shifts the line runs and the separate machines you no longer need to buy and connect.

### How Fast Can a Case Packer Change Over Between SKUs?

It depends on what changes between the SKUs. On TRFOnepack, robotic loading supports multiple products, recipes and packing patterns within the validated application envelope, so a product or pattern change starts with a stored recipe. A new case size also involves the erecting and sealing steps, so TRF defines the case format range and the changeover plan before the design is confirmed.

### What Is the Difference Between a Case Packer and a Case Erector?

A case erector forms a flat corrugated blank into an open case; a case packer loads products into that case. TRFOnepack does both and seals the case as well, in one machine. TRF does not sell stand-alone case erectors: case forming exists inside TRFOnepack and inside [end-of-line integration](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/end-of-line-integration/) projects that TRF engineers around its case packing platforms.

### Can Case Packing Equipment Integrate With My Existing Palletizer and Conveyors?

Yes. Because TRFOnepack seals the case, what leaves the machine is a finished shipping unit ready for conveying. TRF engineers the interfaces to conveyors and palletizers already in your plant, and TRFIntraflow covers the integration layer with sensors, PLC and HMI, machine communication and palletizer interfaces. Existing equipment and controls are reviewed with your team before the scope is defined.
