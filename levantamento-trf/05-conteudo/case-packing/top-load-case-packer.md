## What Is a Top Load Case Packer?

It is secondary packaging equipment that fills a shipping case through its open top. The case stands with its flaps open, products are grouped into the required pack pattern, and the machine lowers or drops them into the case in a controlled way. The full case then moves on to closing, sealing, conveying and palletizing.

The format is named for the direction of loading. Products travel vertically into the case, so the machine can place them in rows, in layers, flat or on edge. That is why one format works for very different packages: a robot can set a delicate thermoformed tray down gently, while a gravity system can collate a group of flexible bags and release it in one controlled motion.

Most top-load lines follow the same sequence, whatever the loading technology: an empty case is formed from a flat blank and presented open; packages arrive from primary packaging and are spaced, oriented or grouped; the group is placed into the case from above; the flaps are closed and sealed; and the sealed case leaves toward coding, conveying and palletizing.

For most plants, the practical question is not whether to top load but how. The answer depends on how many SKUs the line runs, how products arrive, whether case erecting and sealing already exist, the floor space available and the products per minute the line needs. ## How Do Pack Patterns and Changeovers Work on a Top Load Case Packer?

The pack pattern is the arrangement of products inside the case: how many units, in how many rows and layers, and in which orientation. It is a key input for a top load case packer, because it sets the number of picks or drops per case, the collation the infeed must perform and the case dimensions.

- **Count and layers.** Multi-layer patterns add a placement step per layer, which affects cycle time on robotic systems.
- **Orientation.** Products can lie flat, stand on edge or sit at an angle, depending on the package and on how the case is opened later.
- **Fill and stability.** The pattern must fill the case enough to protect the product in transport without overfilling it.
- **Retail requirements.** Some customers specify the pattern and the case, and the packer must deliver it consistently across shifts.

Changeover capability differs by platform. TRFRapidpack changes products and patterns by recipe, with reduced mechanical adjustments, which makes it the platform for lines with frequent SKU changes. TRFOnepack runs multiple products, recipes and packing patterns through robotic loading within the validated application. TRFDroppack is designed for standardized pack patterns, where changeovers are less frequent and a simpler machine has fewer parts to maintain.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is the Difference Between a Case Packer and a Case Erector?

A case erector forms an open case from a flat blank, and a case packer loads products into that case. TRF supplies case erecting only as part of an integrated solution: TRFOnepack erects, packs and seals in one machine, and end-of-line projects include case forming and closing, so one partner answers for the flow from flat case to sealed case.

### What Is a Drop Packer, and How Does Gravity Packing Work?

A drop packer collates products above an open case and loads them through a controlled drop instead of picking each one with a robot. TRFDroppack uses this principle for pillow bags, pouches, VFFS and other flexible packages. It fits when the product tolerates gravity loading and the pattern repeats, which keeps mechanical complexity low and removes robotic end-of-arm tooling from maintenance.

### How Many Products per Minute Can a Robotic Case Packer Run?

TRFRapidpack, the robotic platform, is designed for applications of about 100 to 200 products per minute. TRF publishes application ranges, not machine speeds: actual capacity depends on the product, the pick tool, the pack pattern, the case and the infeed, and TRF engineering validates it for each line before the design is closed.

### Does a Case Packer Need Vision-Guided Robotics for High-Mix Production?

Only when product position varies. TRFRapidpack picks with vision-guided delta robots when products arrive in a random flow, and with indexed picking when they arrive in a controlled position. High-mix lines often benefit from vision because it removes mechanical re-orientation at every SKU change, while repetitive formats can run on indexed picking or gravity loading.

### Can Case Packing Equipment Integrate With My Existing Palletizer and Conveyors?
