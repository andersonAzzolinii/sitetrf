# Talk With a TRF Sales Engineer About Your Line.

Tell us about your line, and a Sales Engineer prepares the right conversation for your project.

Source: https://trfautomation.com/contact/

Contact TRF Industrial Automation through the form on this page or by phone at (913) 242-8019. Tell us what you produce, how fast the line runs and which part of the end of the line you want to automate, and a TRF Sales Engineer will contact you to schedule a conversation about the application.

## What Happens After You Send the Form?

A TRF Sales Engineer reviews your answers and contacts you to schedule a conversation about your line. The more concrete the description, the faster the answer: "we palletize 24 lb cases at about 12 per minute on two shifts" moves a conversation further than "we need a palletizer".
