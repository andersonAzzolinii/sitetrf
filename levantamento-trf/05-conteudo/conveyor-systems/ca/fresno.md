## What Is the Difference Between a Sanitary Conveyor and a Standard Industrial Conveyor?

A sanitary conveyor is designed around cleaning and food contact, not only transport. It carries exposed food between processing, inspection and packaging, with open construction that is easy to sanitize. A standard industrial conveyor moves packaged goods, such as sealed cases, cartons, trays and totes, where flow, routing and accumulation matter more than washdown.

TRF covers both with two platforms. [TRFCleanflow](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/hygienic-food-conveyor/) is the sanitary platform for exposed food, and [TRFIntraflow](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/case-conveyor-intralogistics/) is the modular platform for packaged goods moving between packaging, case packing, palletizing and the warehouse.

## What to Prepare Before Requesting a Technical Consultation in Fresno

An application review moves faster when a few details are ready in advance:

- **Products conveyed:** cases, cartons, trays, totes or exposed food, with dimensions and typical weight.
- **Flow map:** where product enters and exits the system, and which machines sit at each end.
- **Rates:** average and peak volume per line.
- **Environment and sanitation:** whether the area is dry, low-moisture or washed down, and the cleaning routine used today.
- **Layout:** available floor space, level changes and access for maintenance and sanitation.
- **Existing equipment:** any case packer, palletizer or warehouse system the conveyor interfaces with.

### How Does Zero-Pressure Accumulation Work on a Case Conveyor?

Zero-pressure accumulation divides the conveyor into zones that start and stop individually, so units accumulate without pressing against each other. When the downstream machine is ready, zones release in sequence. It protects products and cases, helps prevent jams at transfers and gives a palletizer a predictable infeed. TRFIntraflow includes zero-pressure accumulation, metering and gapping modules.
