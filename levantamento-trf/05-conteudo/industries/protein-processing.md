## What Do Protein Plants Convey, Pack and Palletize?

At the end of the line, protein plants pack trays, thermoformed packages and bags into shipping cases, move the cases to the palletizer and palletize cases and boxes, including in frozen areas. This table maps each step to the TRF platform that handles it.

| Step in a protein plant | What moves | TRF platform |
|---|---|---|
| Primary packaging | Trays, thermoformed packages and bags | Interface only: TRF connects to the machine already installed |
| Case packing | Packaged product into shipping cases | TRFRapidpack, TRFDroppack or TRFOnepack |
| Case flow and buffering | Full cases between packing and palletizing | TRFIntraflow |
| Palletizing outside frozen areas | Cases, boxes, trays and bags | TRFCobot, TRFCompact, TRFSpeed or a custom palletizer |
| Palletizing below 32°F | Cases in frozen areas | TRFSubzero |
| Wrapping, labeling and warehouse handoff | Finished pallets | [End-of-line integration](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/end-of-line-integration/) |

Protein plants palletize several unit types, each with its own page: [case palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/case-palletizer/) for shipping cases, [box palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/box-palletizer/) for boxed product, [tray palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/tray-palletizer/) for trays, [bag palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/bag-palletizer/) for bagged product and [bundle palletizer](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/bundle-palletizer/) for shrink-wrapped multipacks.

## How Does Case Conveying Affect the End of a Protein Line?

Conveyor design decides whether a short stop at one machine stops the area upstream. [TRFIntraflow](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/case-conveyor-intralogistics/) takes over once product is in a case.

Accumulation, zero-pressure accumulation, metering and gapping give the line a buffer, and merge, divert, sorting and reject modules route cases from several packing lines to the right palletizing point. TRF engineers the interface with the case packer and the palletizer, so conveying, packing and palletizing run as one material-flow system.

## How Do You Choose a Case Packer for Packaged Protein Products?

Start with the package. TRFRapidpack packs trays, thermoformed packages and pouches into the case from the top with a robot, and changes products and patterns through recipes with reduced mechanical adjustments. TRFDroppack loads flexible and IQF bags with a controlled drop, which suits repetitive bag patterns. TRFOnepack erects, packs and seals the case in one machine, which suits smaller lines that want fewer machines.

Rate, pack pattern and floor space decide among the three, so TRF evaluates all of them for each product and confirms the platform with your own packages before selection. Compare the options on the [case packing](https://trfautomation.com/case-packing/) page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What End-of-Line Equipment Does a Protein Plant Need?

A protein plant needs a top-load case packer to load packaged trays, thermoformed packages or bags into shipping cases, case conveyors with accumulation and routing, and a palletizer at the end. TRF supplies TRFRapidpack, TRFDroppack or TRFOnepack for case packing, TRFIntraflow for case conveying and a palletizer chosen by cases per minute, case weight and floor space.

### Can Palletizing Equipment Operate Below Freezing for Frozen Protein?

Yes, with equipment built for it. TRFSubzero is the TRF palletizer for areas below 32°F, and frozen areas are among its target applications. TRF validates each application with your product, end-of-arm tool and pallet pattern.

### How Does Automation Help With Labor Shortages in Meat and Poultry Plants?

It removes the most repetitive positions at the end of the line: manual case loading and pallet building. Those operators can move to quality checks or line supervision, and output depends less on how many people are available for a given shift. Automation also reduces repeated lifting, bending and twisting.
