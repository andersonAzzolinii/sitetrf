## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Palletizer?

A palletizer is a machine that stacks packed units, such as cases, bags or trays, onto a pallet in a defined pattern at the end of a production line. A robotic palletizer does it with a robot arm and an end-of-arm tool, building each layer automatically so operators no longer lift every case by hand. The [robotic palletizing page](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/) compares the TRF platforms.

### What Is Palletization?

Palletization is the process of building a stable pallet load from cases, bags or other packed units. It covers the pallet pattern, the number of layers, slip sheets between layers when the load needs them, and the discharge of the finished pallet to wrapping and shipping. A palletizer automates that process at the end of the line, layer by layer.

### What Is the Typical Payback Period for a Robotic Palletizer?

It depends on your line. Payback is driven by the labor hours the palletizer replaces, shifts per day, local labor cost and the installed cost of the system. The [palletizing ROI calculator](https://trfautomation.com/tools/palletizing-roi-calculator/) applies this method to your own line data.
