## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a Robotic Palletizer Operate Below Freezing (32°F)?

Yes, when the cell is engineered for the cold. TRFSubzero is the TRF platform for palletizing in areas below 32°F, for frozen food, ice cream and refrigerated or frozen areas. The Custom Foods case study documents a robotic palletizing system engineered for cold storage, with reported gains in end-of-line throughput, stable operation regardless of temperature and better operator safety and ergonomics.

### Can One Automation Partner Supply Palletizing, Case Packing and Conveyors?

Yes. TRF engineers palletizing, case packing and conveying as one end-of-line system, supported from Lenexa, Kansas. The Lodge Cast Iron case shows an ergonomic case packing station and robotic palletizing scoped as a single project, and the Avon case shows conveying, case handling and vertical transport engineered as one material flow.

### What Is the Typical Payback Period for a Robotic Palletizer?

Payback depends on the labor hours the palletizer replaces, shifts per day, local labor cost and the installed cost of the system, so another plant's result does not transfer to yours. A case study shows that an application class is solved; the [palletizing ROI calculator](https://trfautomation.com/tools/palletizing-roi-calculator/) gives an initial estimate from your own data, and a TRF Sales Engineer completes it.
