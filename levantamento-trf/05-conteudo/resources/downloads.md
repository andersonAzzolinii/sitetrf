## What Data Should You Collect Before Talking to a Supplier?

- Product and package type, with dimensions in inches and weight in lb, including the heaviest SKU.
- Rate in cases or products per minute, today and at the volume you plan for.
- Number of lines feeding the end of the line, and shifts per day.
- Pallet type, pallet pattern and load height.
- Available floor space and a simple sketch of the area.
- Environment: areas below 32°F, dry-clean or washdown.
- Equipment upstream and downstream, such as conveyors, labelers and wrappers.

With this data, a TRF Sales Engineer can tell you which platform to look at first and what has to be validated before a quote.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is EOAT and How Does It Affect Palletizer Payload?

EOAT is the end-of-arm tool: the gripper, clamp, fork or vacuum head at the end of the robot arm that holds the case. Its own weight counts against the robot's payload, together with the case weight and, for multi-case picks, the combined weight of the cases. TRF validates the final payload with your product, gripper, pallet pattern and reach.

### Can a Conveyor System Integrate With My Palletizer and Case Packer?

Yes, and that is how TRF supplies conveyors: as engineered systems integrated into the line, never as loose components. TRFIntraflow modules handle conveying, accumulation, routing and elevation between machines, and [end-of-line integration](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/end-of-line-integration/) covers case packing, conveying and palletizing as one flow with one partner responsible.
