## How Do You Plan a First End-of-Line Automation Project?

Define the problem first, collect line data second and choose equipment third.

1. **Define the problem, not the machine.** Repetitive manual lifting, operators tied to the end of the line, a bottleneck that limits packaging or a growth plan the current line cannot absorb lead to different systems.
2. **Collect line data.** Products and packages, case dimensions and weights, rate in cases or products per minute, pallet patterns, shifts, floor space, temperature of the area and the equipment upstream and downstream.
3. **Shortlist by line setup and speed.** The [robotic palletizing page](https://trfautomation.com/robotic-palletizing/) shows which platform fits each speed and load; the [end-of-line integration page](https://trfautomation.com/conveyor-systems/end-of-line-integration/) covers projects that join packing, conveying and palletizing.
4. **Estimate payback.** The [palletizing ROI calculator](https://trfautomation.com/tools/palletizing-roi-calculator/) gives an initial estimate from your shifts, labor and the investment you want to test.
5. **Ask for an application review.** A TRF Sales Engineer validates product, end-of-arm tool, pallet pattern, reach and process before quoting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is the Difference Between a Cobot Palletizer and a Traditional Robotic Palletizer?

A cobot palletizer uses a collaborative robot, an arm designed for shared workspaces and moderate speeds, in a compact cell with a lower entry cost. A traditional robotic palletizer uses an industrial robot that moves faster, carries heavier loads and runs higher duty cycles. TRFCobot covers lower-speed, high-mix lines; TRFCompact and TRFSpeed cover higher rates and payloads.

### Can a Compact Palletizer Be Installed in a Brownfield Plant With Limited Space?

Yes. TRFCompact puts an industrial robot in a compact standardized cell designed for brownfield plants and lines where production space has high value, at about 5 to 15 cases per minute and cases up to about 44 lb. TRF validates floor space, reach, pallet positions and operator access with your layout before quoting.
