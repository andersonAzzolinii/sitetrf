## What Can You Discuss at a Trade Show Meeting?

The whole end of the line: robotic palletizing, case packing, conveying and how they work together. Start from the problem your plant feels most, and the conversation moves to the platform worth discussing first. The [PACK EXPO International 2026 page](https://trfautomation.com/resources/trade-shows-and-events/pack-expo-international-2026/) lists which platform to ask about for each challenge.

## How Do You Prepare a Productive Trade Show Meeting?

Book the meeting before the show and send basic line data in advance: product and package, rate in cases or products per minute, shifts, pallet pattern, floor space and the equipment upstream and downstream. The data checklist is on the [PACK EXPO International 2026 page](https://trfautomation.com/resources/trade-shows-and-events/pack-expo-international-2026/). Throughput, product data and a photo of the current line are enough for a first platform discussion.

## What Happens After the Show?

Send the line data from the meeting through the contact page and mention the show. A TRF Sales Engineer reviews it, TRF engineering validates the application (product, end-of-arm tool, pallet pattern, reach and process) and the quote follows from that review. To put a first number on payback, run the [palletizing ROI calculator](https://trfautomation.com/tools/palletizing-roi-calculator/).

### Who From Your Plant Should Attend a Supplier Meeting?

The people who will own the decision: the plant or operations manager for throughput and labor, engineering for layout and integration, maintenance for access and support, and procurement or finance for scope and payback. If only one person travels, collect the other departments' questions beforehand so the meeting answers them all.

### Can You Discuss a Frozen or Washdown Application at a Trade Show?

Yes. Bring the actual temperature of the area, whether cases arrive frosted, how often the line is cleaned and with what method. TRFSubzero covers palletizing areas below 32°F, and TRFCleanflow covers exposed food in dry-clean and washdown versions, so those details tell the team which platform and which version to discuss with you.
