## How Should You Prepare for a Meeting With an Automation Supplier at a Trade Show?

Write the problem in one sentence, collect the data that describes your line and agree with your team on what a good answer looks like. Send the data in advance through TRF's PACK EXPO page or the [contact form](https://trfautomation.com/contact/), mentioning PACK EXPO International 2026, so the meeting becomes a working session:

- Product and package type, dimensions in inches and weight in lb, including the heaviest SKU
- Current and target throughput, in cases per minute or products per minute
- Number of lines feeding the end of line, and shifts per day
- Pallet type, pattern and load height, with photos of a finished pallet
- Available floor space in feet and a sketch of the current end of line
- Environment: areas below 32°F, dry or washdown zones
- What sits upstream and downstream: fillers, baggers, checkweighers, labelers, wrappers

For palletizing, bring the exact case dimensions and weight, plus photos of the floor space where the cell would sit. For case packing, bring samples when you can, because the behavior of a flexible package is hard to describe on paper. For conveying, mark where products wait, where operators intervene and where the line stops most often.

### Can a Robotic Palletizer Operate Below Freezing (32°F)?

Yes, when the cell is engineered for it: the robot must be designed for sub-zero operation, and the components around it developed for frozen environments. TRFSubzero is the TRF palletizing system for areas below 32°F, used in frozen food, ice cream, and refrigerated and frozen areas. Bring the temperature of your palletizing area to the booth.

### Can a Palletizer Be Integrated With My Existing Conveyor and Wrapping Line?

Yes. The infeed, accumulation, pallet handling and discharge to wrapping are engineered with the palletizer, and the controls interface with the equipment that stays in place. When several machines are involved, end-of-line integration puts one partner in charge of the complete flow from packaging to pallet. Bring a layout sketch of your current line to the meeting.
