## What Is a Robotic Case Palletizer, and How Is It Different From a Cobot Palletizer?

A robotic case palletizer is an automated cell in which a robot picks shipping cases from the end of a packaging line and stacks them on pallets in a programmed pattern, replacing manual or semi-automated pallet building. The same equipment is often called an automatic case palletizer or a robotic palletizing cell.

Every robotic case palletizer has the same basic parts: an infeed conveyor that presents the cases, a robot that moves them, an end-of-arm tool (EOAT) that grips them, one or more pallet positions and a control system that stores recipes and pallet patterns.

What changes from one cell to another is the robot and the scale. A cobot palletizer uses a collaborative robot for lower rates and lighter cases. A compact palletizer uses an industrial robot, with the speed, payload and duty cycle that come with it, in a cell designed to occupy as little floor as possible. High-speed systems go further, serving several lines at once and handling pallets, slip sheets and discharge automatically.

TRFCompact holds the middle position. Compared with a large robotic cell, the difference is the footprint and the project: the cell is standardized and compact, so it is easier to deploy, configure and scale, and the project work concentrates on your products, your layout and your pallet flow.
